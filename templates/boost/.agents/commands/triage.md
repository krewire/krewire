Triage the reported issue.

1. Load the `bug-triage` skill.
2. Reproduce the issue (steps, environment), classify severity/priority and category, and locate the suspected area.
3. Produce one actionable outcome: either a fix task (with a failing-test-first suggestion) or the clarifying questions the reporter must answer.

Report: reproduction status, severity/priority, category, suspected area, and recommended action.
